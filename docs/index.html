The apps

Each one is its own app, built on the same fast core.

Photosplash

Photo editor

Coming soon to Flathub

Layer-based photo editing that feels instantly familiar. Paint with your pen tablet, blend layers on the GPU and tweak with live previews.

  • 24 blend modes rendered on the GPU, in perceptual or linear light.
  • Pressure-sensitive brushes with size and opacity from your pen.
  • Selections and Free Transform: marquee, lasso, magic wand, add, subtract, intersect.
  • Adjustments and filters like Levels, Curves, Hue/Saturation and blurs, with live preview.
  • Full undo history and the fast, compact .jdp document format.
  • Photoshop-compatible shortcuts so there is nothing to relearn.

Cinematica

Video editor

In development

A timeline editor in the spirit of the classics, built on the same renderer and UI toolkit as Photosplash.

  • Multi-track timeline for video and audio clips.
  • Pro edits: insert, overwrite, lift, extract, razor, ripple and slip.
  • Frame-accurate playback and scrubbing, powered by FFmpeg.
  • Snapshot undo for every edit.

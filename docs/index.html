Photo and video tools, made for Linux.

jamsdesign is a small family of native creative apps written in Rust. They are fast, they work with your pen tablet, and they use the shortcuts you already know.

See the apps View the source

Gallerion

Photo editor

Layer-based photo editing that feels familiar from the first click. Paint with your pen, blend layers on the GPU and see every adjustment live.

Coming soon to Flathub

  • 24 blend modes, rendered on the GPU in perceptual or linear light.
  • Pressure-sensitive brushes that take size and opacity from your pen.
  • Selections and Free Transform with marquee, lasso and magic wand.
  • Adjustments and filters like Levels, Curves and Hue/Saturation, with live preview.
  • Full undo history and the compact .jdp document format.
  • Photoshop-compatible shortcuts, so there is nothing to relearn.

Cinematica

Video editor

A timeline editor in the spirit of the classics, built on the same renderer and interface as Gallerion.

In development

  • Multi-track timeline for video and audio clips.
  • Professional edits: insert, overwrite, lift, extract, razor, ripple and slip.
  • Frame-accurate playback and scrubbing, powered by FFmpeg.
  • Undo for every edit, back to the first cut.

Why another creative suite?

It feels native

Pure Rust and a wgpu renderer. Big images stay smooth because pixel work is tiled and runs in parallel.

It respects your tablet

Pressure and tilt on Wayland, including KDE, with no extra setup.

Nothing to relearn

Tools, menus and shortcuts sit where years of muscle memory expect them.

One family

Every app shares the same colour, document and interface code, so moving between them is easy.

More apps will join the shelf over time.

Install

Flatpak packages for Flathub are on the way. Until then, you can build the apps from source with stable Rust and a Vulkan-capable GPU. Cinematica also needs FFmpeg. 

git clone https://github.com/jamesisboredcoding/jamsdesign
cd jamsdesign/gallerion
cargo run --release -p gallerion
